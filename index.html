🌱 Where this fits

In She Sells™, every lesson comes with its own prompt. You copy it, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, and get one post. That's how you learn each idea, one at a time.

Your Marketing Bestie is where it all comes together. The Playbook holds everything from the lessons and prompts in one file. Add your Client Mirror Snapshot and your Human Design, and your AI knows the rules, your buyer, and you, every time you write.

  • The prompts help you write one post while you learn the idea behind it.
  • Your Bestie uses everything you've learned, any time you ask. No copying prompts.
  • Your Snapshot and Human Design make every post sound like it's for your buyer, and from you.

Keep using the prompts whenever you want to practice one idea. Your Bestie is for every day. 💖

Paste in the instructions

These tell your AI to follow the Playbook every time you ask for help with a post, and to talk to you like a bestie.

Already have instructions in your Project? Keep them. Paste these underneath what's already there, so your AI keeps everything it knows and adds your Bestie on top.

MY MARKETING BESTIE Add this to everything else in my instructions for this Project. Keep following all of it. When I ask for help with a promo, a post, a title, or a comment for Skool, you're my Marketing Bestie. Before you write, read the She Promotes™ Promo Playbook in this Project and follow it. If my About Me, my Client Mirror Snapshot, my Human Design chart, or my Promote By Design blueprint are in this Project, read those too and use them. Who you are: the friend who's really good at marketing. Warm, a little witty, and honest in a kind way. Talk to me like a person, never like a tool or a textbook. How you help me: - Write every post in my voice, using my words and the way I talk. Your personality shows up in how you talk to me, never in my post. - After each post, add one short Bestie Note on the psychology behind one choice you made, in everyday words. - Tell me the truth kindly. If something could be in anyone's post, show me something better. - When something's working, tell me what it is and why. - Never invent results, numbers, prices, testimonials, or quotes. If something's missing, ask me. - Format every post so I can copy and paste it straight into Skool: Unicode bold for headings, no markdown, no hashtags, no em dashes. - End by asking: "Want to go deeper, or is this good?" If something here doesn't match my other instructions, ask me which one I want before you write.

5

Say hi to your Bestie

Open a new chat inside your Project and send this first. She'll tell you what she already knows about you, what's missing, and everything she can help with.

Hi Bestie! 👋 I just set you up. Look at everything in this Project and tell me, in a few short lines, what you already know about me, my business, and my buyer, and what's missing. Then show me the menu of things you can help me with, and ask me what I want to work on first.

Anytime you're not sure what to ask, just type menu and she'll show you the list again.

6

Things to ask your Bestie

Copy one, paste it into your Project, and fill in the [brackets]. Or just talk to her like a friend. Short and messy is fine. She'll ask for anything she needs. 💖

✍️ Promote my offer
Write a promo for my [offer]. It's for [who it's for]. The link goes to [where the link goes].
🏡 Invite people to my community
Write a promo inviting people to join my community, [community name]. This week inside we're [what's happening]. Here's the link: [link].
🛍️ Promote a product
Write a promo for my [product]. It's perfect for [who and when they'd use it]. The price is [price] and the link is [link].
📅 Promote an event or workshop
Write a post for my [free workshop, live, or challenge] on [date and time]. They'll walk away with [what they'll get]. Sign up here: [link].
🔍 Check my post before I share it
Check this post before I share it. Tell me what's working and what might make someone pause, then give it back cleaned up: [paste your post]
🎯 Give me title ideas
Give me 5 titles for my post about [what you're promoting], each one using a different title starter.
💡 Share what I know (no link)
Give me 5 tip ideas I could share this week with no link, based on what my buyer is going through. Then write the one I pick.
📚 A lesson post for another group
Turn this into a lesson post I can share in a group that doesn't allow promos: [your tip or idea]
👋 My introduction post
Write my introduction post for [community name]. Make it interesting enough that people want to click and get to know me.
🗓️ Plan my promos for the week
Help me plan my promos for this week. I'm promoting [your offers] and I post in [your groups and days]. Give me a different angle for each day.
💬 Help me write a real comment
Help me write a supportive comment on this post that shows what I know, with no pitch and no link: [paste the post]
🎨 Change the feel of a post
Make this post more [fun, heartfelt, bold, or calm] and keep my voice: [paste your post]

After every post, she adds a Bestie Note on why it works and asks if you want to go deeper. Say yes, and she'll give you a few ways to make it even better.
